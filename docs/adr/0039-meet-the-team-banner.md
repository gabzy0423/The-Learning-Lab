# Create a Meet The Team banner

The standalone `/about/team` page will adopt the existing About banner pattern: a contained wide image with rounded corners, a controlled charcoal overlay, centered white copy, and responsive cover behavior with a stronger overlay on smaller screens.

The generated image will show a generic diverse team collaborating in a learning or work environment. It will not represent approved staff, include identifying biographies, contain logos or embedded text, or imply verified organizational claims.

The banner copy will remain proposed:

- Eyebrow: `Meet The Team`
- Heading: `The people behind The Learning Lab.`
- Supporting line: `A closer look at the people, perspectives, and energy behind our work.`

**Status:** accepted
