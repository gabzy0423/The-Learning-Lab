# Plan the Impacting Strategy category banner

The Impacting Strategy page will use a dedicated category banner visual that communicates strategic planning rather than generic workplace collaboration.

Banner visual direction:

- Show a small strategy-planning scene with a maximum of three people reviewing a roadmap, planning board, or high-level business framework.
- Include subtle non-readable strategy cues such as roadmap lines, connected nodes, charts, or planning markers without fake data or text.
- Use a wide landscape composition with the people and strategy visual positioned on the right and calm negative space on the left for the existing white banner copy.
- Use premium photorealistic corporate editorial styling, natural daylight, and a charcoal/teal/mint-compatible palette.
- Avoid large groups, readable text, logos, watermarks, and generic collaboration imagery.
