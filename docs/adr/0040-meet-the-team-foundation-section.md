# Add a Foundation section to Meet The Team

The standalone `/about/team` page will place a new `Foundation` section directly below the Meet The Team banner and before the `Our people` placeholder grid. The section will use a two-column layout: neutral Foundation intro copy on the left and a numbered Accordion on the right, matching the existing `Who we are` alignment pattern.

The section will use four proposed principles derived from existing Learning Lab language:

- Human-centered
- Practical
- Collaborative
- Growth-minded

Each item will have proposed supporting copy, remain clearly unverified until stakeholder approval, and use the existing Accordion behavior: all items closed by default, one open at a time, keyboard accessible, and reduced-motion compatible.

**Status:** accepted
