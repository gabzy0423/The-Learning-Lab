# Add a proposed detail page for Communicate with Impact

The first Personal Development card, **Communicate with Impact**, will link to a dedicated detail route:

`/programs/personal-development/communicate-with-impact`

The card becomes clickable across its image, title, and content area, with keyboard focus support. Other Personal Development cards remain non-clickable until their course routes and content are confirmed.

The detail page uses an informative editorial structure:

- Breadcrumb/category context
- Course title and short lead
- Large course image
- Proposed Copy notice
- About this course overview
- What you will explore
- Practical workplace application
- Closing `Talk to us` CTA

All long-form copy is Proposed Copy derived from the existing short description: building confidence, authority, and tact in discussions through assertive communication techniques for working effectively with different people and situations. The page must not invent duration, instructor, price, certification, testimonials, or guaranteed outcomes.
