# Plan Managing Work around consistent placeholder cards

The Managing Work page will use five cards in the same visual system as Personal Development: numbered marker, square blank image frame, approved title, concise Proposed Copy description, subtle brand accent, and a muted non-clickable `Learn more` label. The cards will use a three-column desktop, two-column tablet, and one-column mobile grid. No sixth placeholder card, image, or route will be added until approved content exists.

Card order and Proposed Copy:

1. **Complete Staff Work** — Build a structured approach to preparing complete recommendations, coordinating details, and helping teams make informed decisions.
2. **Practical Project Management** — Strengthen the planning, coordination, and delivery habits needed to move projects forward with clarity and confidence.
3. **Innovation & Design Thinking** — Explore practical ways to understand challenges, generate ideas, and shape solutions around real needs.
4. **The Problem-Solving Toolkit** — Use practical tools to break down complex challenges, evaluate options, and move toward workable solutions.
5. **Managing Meetings** — Plan and facilitate focused meetings that encourage participation, clarify decisions, and end with clear next steps.
