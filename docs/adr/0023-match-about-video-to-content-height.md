# Match the About video to content height

On desktop, the About video frame will stretch to the height of the expanded left content block so the two sides share aligned top and bottom edges and comparable visual energy. The video will use `object-contain` over a subtle Charcoal backdrop so the full source frame remains visible; letterbox space is preferred over cropping important content. Mobile and tablet will preserve the `3:2` video ratio and content-driven stacking. The About copy and spacing remain unchanged.
