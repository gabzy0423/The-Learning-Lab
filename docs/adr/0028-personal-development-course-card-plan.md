# Plan Personal Development around ordered course image cards

The Personal Development category page will use a focused category intro followed by six course-card image slots, mapped in filename order from `perso1.png` through `perso6.png`. The cards will reuse the Signature Learning Portfolio treatment: the established card proportions, a square image frame with `contain` behavior so the complete square assets remain visible, a subtle full-width low-contrast divider between image and description, image overlay, and hover/focus image-swap behavior where an alternate asset exists. The responsive grid will use three columns on desktop, two on tablet, and one on mobile.

The supplied descriptions will follow the same filename order for `perso1.png` through `perso5.png` and will be shortened to one or two readable sentences without adding claims. The sixth card remains on hold because its supplied description duplicates the fifth. Original approved course titles must be used; no title will be inferred from a description. Until titles and destinations are confirmed, cards remain non-clickable and do not create course routes.

Approved card copy order:

1. Build confidence, authority, and tact in discussions through assertive communication techniques for working effectively with different people and situations.
2. Discover better ways to work in a technology-driven workplace by learning how to leverage technology for yourself and your team.
3. Develop practical presentation skills through application, feedback, and coaching to communicate ideas clearly and achieve stronger outcomes.
4. Build a professional image that strengthens confidence, credibility, and working relationships across generations in today’s workplace.
5. Learn practical strategies to manage stress, support wellbeing, and improve productivity at work and in personal life using the Six Dimensions framework.
6. Learn how to recognize when you or your employees may be struggling, support open workplace dialogue, and develop a growth mindset that strengthens your Adversity Quotient.
