# Add an Our Services section to the homepage

The homepage will add a compact `Our Services` section immediately after the Signature Learning Portfolio and before the practical methodology section. It will introduce the two Digital Solutions destinations:

- `Custom Human Resource Information System (HRIS)` → `/solutions/hris`
- `Competency Platform` → `/solutions/competency-platform`

The section will use three equal cards on desktop and stack them on mobile. Its visual treatment will be distinct from the learning-program cards while remaining within the shared page container: white cards with a soft border and shadow, a number badge in the upper-left, a Mint/Turquoise icon tile in the upper-right, a prominent title, a neutral description, three concise proposed-direction checklist bullets with Mint/Turquoise check icons, a divider, and a `Learn more` action. The checklist copy will remain neutral and derived from each service name rather than presenting unapproved capabilities as confirmed features.
