# Hero Visual Specification

## Status

Context-engineered direction. Ready for implementation after explicit confirmation.

The hero visual is a proposed asset until its source rights and public use are approved. It must not imply that the people shown are The Learning Lab staff, clients, or testimonial subjects.

## Purpose

Support the homepage Hero message, “Learning that moves people forward,” with a credible visual representation of learning and development for teams, leaders, and organizations.

## Visual direction

- Photorealistic people from the existing concept/reference material; their identities and relationship to The Learning Lab remain unverified.
- Four to five diverse working adults in a modern corporate workshop setting.
- Candid collaboration: discussion, listening, sharing ideas, and visible engagement.
- Modern business-casual clothing with simple notebooks or laptops.
- No visible logos, readable text, watermarks, dashboards, or product interfaces.
- Warm natural light with an eye-level editorial photography feel.
- Softly blurred, visually simple office background.
- Square 1:1 source composition for reliable circular cropping.
- Faces and key gestures remain safely inside the crop; no awkward head or hand cropping.
- Image is placed in a circular frame on the right side of the desktop Hero.
- Circular treatment uses a Deep Purple ring with a subtle Mint accent.
- The screenshot reference informs composition and mood only; faces, labels, exact photo, and layout are not copied.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop: image occupies the right Hero column beside the copy.
- Mobile: image stacks below the Hero content instead of disappearing.
- Image text and labels remain separate HTML elements for accessibility and future editing.

## Brand and content boundaries

- Use the existing Deep Purple, Mint, Orange, and Coral palette in the surrounding frame and accents.
- Do not bake slogans, claims, labels, or logos into the image.
- Any decorative labels must be authored as approved HTML copy, not generated into the image.
- The image is a proposed Brand Asset until stakeholder approval and rights/use confirmation.
- It is a Hero Image, not a Course Visual and not evidence of a specific program or service.

## Source decision

Use the hero photo extracted from the existing HTML concept reference as the current proposed asset. Replace it with an approved organization-provided photograph or licensed image if rights or stakeholder approval are not confirmed; the surrounding layout contract remains unchanged.
